# Micro Playable price rationale

Reviewed October 7, 2026. Internal commercial reasoning, not public evidence.

The owner requests a public one-action package and delegates choosing its price.
Implementation selects **Starting at $299 USD** with supplied static artwork,
one tap before the CTA, one visual response, one creative/language, and one
consolidated copy/visual adjustment round. A 1-2 business-day planning estimate
starts after approved complete inputs and is confirmed in the proposal. These
are scoped implementation choices, not independently confirmed market averages.

Primary vendor-page comparison:

- MRAID.io currently lists Similar ad at $290, Unique ad at $590, and Complex ad
  at $1,490: https://mraid.io/ . Similar is described as resembling its portfolio;
  it is not an exactly equivalent one-tap service. Its page also describes a
  broader $1,000-$1,500 typical 2D range. Published offers are not transaction
  evidence or a representative market survey.
- Arc Studio publicly lists 2D creatives from $500:
  https://arc-studio.tech/development . Another scope reference, not an exact
  Micro comparator.

$299 gives a separate entry point below the $799 adapted-gameplay offer while
retaining responsive/provider packaging work, source handoff, and Workers demo
delivery. Network builds remain the owner-approved included bonus. Do not add
new original assets, audio production, animation sequences, gameplay loops, or
additional actions to the Micro base scope. Review actual production effort
before expanding the offer; no margin or sales-volume claims are verified.
